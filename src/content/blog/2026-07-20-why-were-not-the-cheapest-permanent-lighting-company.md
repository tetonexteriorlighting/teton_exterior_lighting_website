## What You're Actually Paying For

A cheaper install usually means a generic strip of lights and a phone app with a handful of preset colors. That's not what we build.

Every Teton install includes real, individually addressable bulbs, which means we can program looks a basic system simply can't produce. Here's what that looks like in practice:

- **Custom presets built for your home.** We don't hand you a generic app and wish you luck. We program presets that match your home's brick, trim, and rooflines so the ambiance actually looks intentional, not just bright.
- **Segments for every section of your house.** Your gables, porch, and garage don't have to run the same look. We break your home into segments so each area can do its own thing, or work together as one display.
- **Zone control for each side of the home.** Front yard doing a warm white glow while the backyard patio runs a party scene? That's zone control, and it's set up for you, not something you piece together yourself after we leave.
- **Done-for-you programming and scheduling.** Holiday schedules, sunset triggers, game day colors. We set it up during install so it just works, instead of leaving you to figure out a settings menu on your own.

This is the part that's easy to miss on a quote. You're not just buying lights. You're buying a system that's already dialed in for your specific house.
